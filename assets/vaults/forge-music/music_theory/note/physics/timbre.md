# Timbre

**Timbre** ("tone color") is what makes the same [[pitch]] sound like a piano rather than a violin. Play A4 on both at the same [[loudness]] and you still instantly know which is which — that difference is timbre.

It's determined by the **overtone spectrum**: above the fundamental [[frequency]] that fixes the pitch, an instrument stacks a set of quieter higher frequencies (overtones), and their particular mix is the instrument's sonic fingerprint. Same pitch, same loudness, different overtone mix = different timbre.

Part of the [[physics]] of a note.
