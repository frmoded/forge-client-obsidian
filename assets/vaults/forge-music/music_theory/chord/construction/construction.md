# Chord Construction

This section is about what a chord IS made of: which notes, spaced how, and in what order. Everything here builds outward from the triad — the atomic 3-note chord — through richer stacks, reorderings, and concrete voicings.

- [[triad]] — the basic 3-note chord: a root plus two stacked thirds.
- [[chord_quality]] — the four triad flavors, set by the semitone spacing of those thirds.
- [[root]] — the note a chord is generated from and named after.
- [[chord_tone]] — the notes actually in the chord versus the ones decorating around it.
- [[seventh_chord]] — stack one more third to get a 4-note chord.
- [[extension]] — keep stacking thirds past the 7th (9ths, 11ths, 13ths) for added color.
- [[inversion]] — the same chord with a different chord tone in the bass.
- [[voicing]] — the concrete realization of an abstract chord: octaves, order, doublings.

Try [[experiments/build_triad]] and [[experiments/build_seventh_chord]] to hear these stacks built from a tonic and a quality of your choosing.
