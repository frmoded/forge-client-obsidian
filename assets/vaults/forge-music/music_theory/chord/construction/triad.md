# Triad

A **triad** is the basic 3-note chord: a root plus two stacked thirds — degrees 1, 3, and 5 of a scale. It's the atom everything else in [[chord]] extends: sevenths add a fourth note, extensions add more, inversions reorder it, voicings dress it up — but the triad is where the stack starts.

Stack a third on a note, then another third on top of that, and you have a triad. Press Run on [[experiments/build_triad]] with `tonic="C4"` and `quality="major"` to hear it — then change the tonic and listen to the same shape land somewhere else.

The two thirds don't have to be the same size, and which sizes you pick is exactly what [[chord_quality]] is about. The bottom note of the stack is the [[root]] — but as [[inversion]] shows, "bottom of the stack" and "lowest note you hear" aren't always the same thing.

Once you can build a triad, [[seventh_chord|seventh chords]] are one more stacked third away.
