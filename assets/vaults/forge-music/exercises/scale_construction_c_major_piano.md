# Description

Click the seven notes of the C major scale on the piano, from the tonic
(C4, middle C) up to the seventh (B4). Then click Forge to grade your
attempt.

C major is the all-white-keys scale — no sharps, no flats. Listen for
the W-W-H-W-W-W step pattern as you pick the keys.

Return Call [[scale_construction_exercise]] with tonic="C4", mode="major", student_pitches=student_pitches, widget_type="piano".
