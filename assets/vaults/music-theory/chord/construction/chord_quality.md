# Chord Quality

**Chord quality** is what gives a triad its emotional color — set entirely by how big the two stacked thirds are, in semitones. Four qualities cover the triad:

- **Major** {0, 4, 7} — a major third then a minor third. Bright, resolved.
- **Minor** {0, 3, 7} — a minor third then a major third. Darker.
- **Diminished** {0, 3, 6} — two minor thirds stacked. Tense, unstable.
- **Augmented** {0, 4, 8} — two major thirds stacked. Unsettled, floating.

Same root, four different feelings. Run [[build_triad]] with `tonic="C4"` and try all four `quality` values in turn — notice how only the middle note (the third) and top note (the fifth) move; the [[root]] stays put.

Quality isn't just a triad idea — [[seventh_chord|seventh chords]] have their own richer set of qualities (major 7th, dominant 7th, minor 7th, half-diminished, diminished) built the same way: pick the semitone stack, hear the color.
