# Diatonic Chords

The **diatonic chords** of a key are the seven chords you get by building a triad on each degree of its scale, using only notes from that scale: I ii iii IV V vi vii° in major. This is the default vocabulary of a key — the chords that "belong" without borrowing any notes from outside it.

Run [[chord_progression]] with `key_name="C"`, `mode_name="major"`, `progression=["I", "ii", "iii", "IV", "V", "vi", "vii°"]` to hear all seven, in order, root to root. Notice the pattern of qualities is fixed by the major scale's interval pattern: I, IV, and V come out major; ii, iii, and vi come out minor; vii° comes out diminished — the case and the ° in the numeral are telling you exactly that.

Those seven chords don't function as equals — see [[harmonic_function]] for how they group into three jobs.
