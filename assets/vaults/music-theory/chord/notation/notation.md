# Chord Notation

This section is about how chords get written and named — the symbols performers read, the symbols theorists think in, and the one notation that prises the two apart.

- [[chord_symbol]] — the pitch-specific name: C, Dm7, G7, Bdim. What performers read.
- [[roman_numeral]] — the key-independent, function-based name: I ii iii IV V vi vii°.
- [[slash_chord]] — explicit control of the bass separate from the chord: Fm/C.

Press Run on [[slash_chord]] to hear a chord and its bass note controlled independently.
