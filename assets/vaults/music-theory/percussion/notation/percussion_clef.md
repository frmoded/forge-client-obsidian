# Percussion Clef

Unpitched percussion doesn't get treble or bass clef — those clefs exist to fix which pitch each staff line represents, and a kick drum or snare doesn't have a pitch in the melodic sense. Percussion staves use a **percussion clef** instead: two thick vertical bars carrying no pitch information at all. Its only job is telling a reader "the lines and spaces here are instrument slots, not degrees of a scale."

This vault's engine renders percussion scores with a percussion clef for exactly that reason — see [[to_kit_notation]], which builds the folded kit staff any multi-instrument percussion score can render to.
