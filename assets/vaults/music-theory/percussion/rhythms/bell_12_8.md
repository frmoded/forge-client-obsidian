description: "The 12/8 bell pattern from West African music: one bell line that you can feel in four or in three, depending on where you tap your foot."
---

Seven hits across twelve steps: long, long, short, long, long, long, short. That's the bell. Tap your foot on every third step and it swings in **four**. Tap your foot on every fourth step and it rolls in **three**. Same bell, same notes. You decide where home is.

```forge-rhythm
[[rhythm_pattern_bell_12_8]]
```

This pattern anchors a huge slice of West African drumming and the music that crossed the Atlantic with it. The drummers around the bell are free to shout. The bell never wavers. It's the one guy at the party who knows what time it is.

It's also why a lot of jazz, blues shuffles and slow rock ballads in 12/8 feel the way they do. You've heard this bell without knowing it.

## Challenge

The bell has **seven** hits. Delete one, any one, and listen to what the pattern says now. Some deletions sound like a wounded animal. One or two sound like an entirely different tradition.

Then do the reverse: add a hit on a step the bell skipped. Find the one extra note that doesn't ruin it.
