Five rhythms. Each one is small enough to hold in your head, and each has done more work than most symphonies. Press play, then wreck them.

- [[tresillo]]: the 3+3+2 cell under half the world's dance music.
- [[hemiola]]: two against three, a waltz with its hands tied.
- [[bell_12_8]]: one bell you can feel in four or in three.
- [[clave]]: five hits holding up an entire musical culture.
- [[tha_dhi_gi_na_thom]]: five syllables from South India you speak before you play.

Every note has a playground Beat Box (nothing you do is saved) and a challenge that moves one hit and asks what broke. That is the exercise. Reading about rhythm is like reading about swimming.

Start with tresillo, because the others lean on it.
