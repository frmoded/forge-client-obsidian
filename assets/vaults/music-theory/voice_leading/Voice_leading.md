How individual voices move from one chord to the next — which note goes where, and why some motions sound smooth while others sound awkward or forbidden. Figured bass is the shorthand that made this thinking possible to notate quickly.

- [[figured_bass]] — numbers under a bass note specifying the chord above it, without writing out every note.
- [[voice_leading_triads]] — smooth motion between triads: common tones held, other voices stepping by the smallest distance available.
- [[voice_leading_seventh_chords]] — the same principles, complicated by a fourth voice and the seventh's need to resolve.
- [[parallel_fifths_octaves]] — the classic forbidden motion, and why it flattens the independence between voices.
- [[non_chord_tone_voice_leading]] — how non-chord tones fit into voice-leading rules rather than breaking them.
- [[voice_leading_history]] — where these rules actually come from.
