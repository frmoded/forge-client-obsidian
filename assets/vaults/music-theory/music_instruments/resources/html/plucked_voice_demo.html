Plucked voice: Old and New

The same notes, two voices. Old is the single-oscillator pluck the gadgets had (six harmonics, one fixed spectrum); New is the plucked-string voice (a string as a sum of partials that each decay on their own, a pick, a mute at the end). A mandolin note is a PAIR of strings 5 cents apart: listen for the slow shimmer. Chord plays three notes about 50 ms apart (a strum). Listen on headphones or speakers, not a laptop's.
