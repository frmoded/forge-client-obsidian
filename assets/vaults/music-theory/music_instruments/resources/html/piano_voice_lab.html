Piano voice lab

Choose the sound of the vault's piano by ear. Two settings, A and B, start as the old long voice and the new shorter one. Pick a preset for each, move the sliders, press the keys or the buttons: the sound is the real piano code of the gadgets, with your numbers. When one is right, press Copy these settings and paste the text back: it is the one table that makes the voice, and it goes in as the new piano everywhere.
