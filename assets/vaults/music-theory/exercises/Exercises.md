Graded practice and interactive demos, spanning every concept in [[notes]],
[[scales]], and [[chord]] — not tied to one topic directory, so they live
here instead. Every note either grades an attempt against a real answer or
plays back something you built. Pick a group below by what you want to
practice or hear.

## Scale construction

- [[complete_this_scale_challenge]] — hear the first four notes of C major, work out the rest.
- [[complete_this_scale_submit]] — submit the four notes that finish the C major octave, get note-by-note feedback.
- [[construct_c_major_piano]] — build the whole C major scale, tonic to tonic, on the piano widget.
- [[scale_construction_a_minor_piano]] — build A natural minor on the piano widget.
- [[scale_construction_f_major_piano]] — build F major on the piano widget (one flat).
- [[scale_construction_g_major_piano]] — build G major on the piano widget (one sharp).
- [[scale_quality_quiz]] — multiple-choice: name the scale quality from its interval pattern.

## Chord construction

- [[build_triad]] — build a triad from a tonic and quality, hear it.
- [[build_seventh_chord]] — build a seventh chord or extension, hear it.
- [[chord_inversions]] — hear the same triad reordered across root/first/second inversion.
- [[slash_chord]] — hear a triad voiced over an independent bass note.
- [[chord_progression]] — realize a Roman-numeral progression in a key/mode of your choice.

## Pitch and physics

- [[octave_up]] — name the note one octave above C4; feedback ties name, octave number, and frequency together.
- [[loudness_demo]] — same four snare hits, only the velocity pattern changes.

## Widget and phrase demos

- [[chord_stream_demo]] — pick a canonical progression from a dropdown, hear it played.
- [[melodic_line_demo]] — pick a melodic contour, hear a four-note phrase.
- [[rhythmic_line_demo]] — pick a rhythmic pattern, hear it on one pitch.
- [[piano_play_smoke]] — click keys on a piano widget, hear them played back.
- [[guitar_play_smoke]] — pick notes on a fretboard widget, hear them played back.
