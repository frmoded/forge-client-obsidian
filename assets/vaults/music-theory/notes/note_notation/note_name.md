The **note names** are the seven letters **A–G** that label the "natural" notes, cycling back to A after G. Here are the seven naturals in order from C:

![[notes/resources/images/note_name.svg]]

Notice the mismatch: only **7 letters** for **12 [[pitch_class|pitch classes]]**. That gap is exactly what the [[accidental|accidentals]] fill — the five leftover pitch classes are reached by nudging a letter up or down.
