A **note** is the atom of music: a single sound with a definite pitch. This section looks at what a note is from two complementary angles — the symbols we use to name it, and the physics of the sound itself. The two meet in the middle: "A4" (a name) and 440 Hz (a frequency) are two descriptions of the same thing.

- [[note_notation]] — the **symbolic** side: how we identify, name, and write a note.
- [[physics]] — the **physical / perceptual** side: what a note is as sound.
- [[Music_instruments]] — real strings and keys that turn those symbols and physics into something you can touch.
