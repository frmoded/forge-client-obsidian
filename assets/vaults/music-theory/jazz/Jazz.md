How jazz extends and re-notates the chord and scale vocabulary built up elsewhere in this vault: richer chord voicings, the ii–V–I progression that drives most jazz harmony, and the scale a player reaches for over each chord in real time.

- [[jazz_chord_symbol]] — extended and altered chord symbols (9ths, 11ths, 13ths, altered dominants) as jazz notates them.
- [[jazz_voicing]] — rootless and shell voicings built for a keyboard or guitar player comping in real time, not a four-part chorale.
- [[ii_v_i]] — the single most common jazz progression, and why it shows up in some form in almost every standard.
