# Consonance and Dissonance

Some intervals sound settled and pleasant when played together; others sound tense and clashing. This isn't just taste — it's physics. Consonant intervals have frequencies related by simple whole-number ratios (the [[octave]]'s 2:1 is the simplest of all); dissonant intervals have frequencies close enough together that they interfere with each other — beating — instead of blending.
