# Enharmonic equivalence

**Enharmonic equivalence** is when two different names point at the identical pitch: **C♯ and D♭ are the same sound**. Same value, different spelling — an alias. On the page they're written differently:

![[resources/images/enharmonic.svg]]

…but they sound identical:

![[resources/audio/enharmonic.mp3]]

Which name you write depends on the key and context, not the sound. This is the direct consequence of covering 12 [[pitch_class|pitch classes]] with a 7-[[note_name|letter]] + [[accidental]] scheme: some pitches get reached from two directions.

Part of the [[notation]] of a note.
