Writing two or more independent melodic lines that work together — governed by rules originally developed as a teaching method, long before they became a compositional style in their own right.

- [[species_counterpoint]] — the five graduated exercises (one note against one, two against one, and on) used for centuries to teach independent voice-writing.
- [[invention]] — a short two-voice piece built by developing a single motive in close dialogue between the voices.
- [[fugue]] — a full contrapuntal form: a subject stated in one voice, imitated in the others, developed and combined with itself throughout.
- [[counterpoint_history]] — where species counterpoint as a teaching method actually came from.
