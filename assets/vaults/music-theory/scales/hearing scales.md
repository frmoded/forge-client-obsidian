You've met the scales; now make your ears earn their keep.

Two games. Each has an easy, medium and hard level: 5, 3 or 1 tries per question. Every right answer inside your tries scores a point, and the score lives on the page until you reload.

Name the scale: you hear one, you pick which it is. Words like bright or dark are only tendencies, so listen for the pattern of steps.

```html-embed
scales/resources/html/scale_ear_name_the_scale.html
286
```

Build the scale: you're handed the tonic and a scale name, and you tap the notes it contains. A wrong tap costs you one of your tries.

```html-embed
scales/resources/html/scale_ear_build_the_scale.html
213
```

You're done when you can build major, the three minors and a mode from any note, and tell major from minor by ear.
