# scripts/dev — the real-browser proof for a UI drain

`node scripts/dev/browser-proof.mjs [--before <git-ref>] [--out <dir>]` mounts the **real** `RhythmEditView` (`src/rhythm-edit-view.ts`, bundled with esbuild and a tiny Obsidian shim in `browser/`) in a page that carries **Obsidian's own `app.css`** (read out of the installed app's asar by `asar-cat.mjs`) and the plugin's `styles.css`, with the real Rhythm Box widget in its sandboxed iframe, the real music edition (Verovio) and, behind a local `/compute` route, the real engine (the bundled Pyodide engine, in Node). `measure.ts` drives it through `cdp.mjs` (headless Chrome over the DevTools protocol): real pointer clicks inside the widget iframe, a sampler for flicker and layout jumps, geometry of the pane, screenshots, timings, and the engine's round trips. With `--before <ref>` it builds the same harness from that commit and compares a vault *without* the profile against it — `normalIdenticalToBefore` in `measurements.json` is "no harm to other vaults" in a browser. It is **not Obsidian** (Electron fonts, CodeMirror's block layout, Obsidian's workspace and Pyodide-in-Obsidian are not exercised); say so in the FEEDBACK.

**Wall-clock only.** Every number it reports is a real `performance.now()` in a real Chrome. Virtual time is banned here: nothing uses `Emulation.setVirtualTimePolicy` or fake timers, because a proof about debounce, flicker and engraving latency that runs on a fake clock proves nothing. To adapt it to another surface, copy `browser/entry.ts` (what is mounted), add the new scenario to `measure.ts`, and keep the three rules: the real class, real events, Obsidian's real CSS. Needs Google Chrome (or `CHROME=<binary>`), Obsidian installed, and the music wheels.

**Phrases (M5, drain 2026-10-22-1850).** `measure-phrase.ts` is the same recipe for the phrase tab: the real `mountPhrase` (control strip + live Score), the real music edition, the real engine behind `/phrase`. Build `OUT=<dir>/harness-phrase.js EDITION=music ENTRY=entry-phrase.ts node scripts/dev/browser/build.mjs`, put `app.css` and `plugin-styles.css` in `<dir>` (browser-proof.mjs makes them), then `node scripts/dev/measure-phrase.ts <dir>`. It exits non-zero unless: 8 bars engraved, a tempo mark per bar and part, the `<midi-player>`'s note sequence has a tempo change at every bar start (within 5 ms), Verovio's timeMap puts the bar starts in the same places, a control edit writes the JSON and redraws, a source-bar edit redraws, and the console has no errors. Audio is not played (headless): it measures what the player is given to schedule.

**Pinning a bar (drain 2026-10-24-1100).** `measure-pin.ts` sweeps a grid of points over a phrase tab's and a mix tab's engraving, classifies what the browser says is under each point (notehead, stem, beam, staff line, rest, label, empty), and clicks a few points of every kind in every bar with REAL DevTools pointer events (a move, then press and release at once): first, repeated, and after another bar is pinned; what pinned is read from the DOM. Build `OUT=<dir>/harness-phrase.js EDITION=music ENTRY=entry-phrase.ts node scripts/dev/browser/build.mjs` (with `app.css` and `plugin-styles.css` beside it), then `node scripts/dev/measure-pin.ts <dir>`. It also answers the Ghost question (noteheads with nothing pinned vs pinned, same weight). Exit 1 when a verdict is false.
